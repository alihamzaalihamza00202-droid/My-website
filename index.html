Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Your name, email, phone, address and payment screenshot are used only to process your order, deliver your product and provide support. We never sell your data. Return Policy - 7 Days

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery if the product is wrong, damaged or defective. Step 1: Submit return reason from Returns page. Step 2: Courier will pickup the product.
